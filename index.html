About Vinyl Viewer

Built for people who still love the whole album.

Vinyl Viewer makes it easy to share and explore public Discogs collections through a focused, artwork-first experience.

👥

View friends’ collections

Discover what your friends are spinning, browse their collection and wantlist, and get inspired by their musical taste.

  • Browse by collection, year, or rating
  • Open any album for complete details
